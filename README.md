# Desdemona

> A horror-inspired survival game featuring exploration, combat, parkour, and hidden collectibles.

## About the Project

This is a personal game development project created using **Unity and C#**.

Inspired by horror games such as *Lucius*, *Pacify*, and *Dead by Daylight*, this project combines survival, exploration, and combat into a challenging experience.

Players must explore a mysterious environment, defeat enemies, collect hidden letters, and unlock the final door to escape.

## Gameplay

### Main Objective

Explore the environment, collect all the hidden letters, defeat enemies, and gather enough points to unlock the final door.

### Starting Conditions

Players begin their journey in a forest with:

- **Health:** 100
- **Ammunition:** 50
- **Score:** 0

### Winning the Game

To complete the level, players must:
1. Find and collect all the hidden letters.
2. Defeat enemies and earn points.
3. Collect enough points to unlock the final door.
4. Reach the final exit.

### Losing the Game

If the player's health reaches zero, the game ends and the player must restart the level.

## Level Design

The game takes place across a series of floating islands, featuring forests, platforms, and interconnected areas.

The environment is designed to challenge the player's movement, awareness, and ability to survive.

---

## Combat System

Combat focuses on accuracy and careful ammunition management rather than continuously shooting enemies.

### Player Combat

- Players do not have a permanent aiming reticle.
- Hold the **Right Mouse Button** to aim.
- Shooting speed is relatively slow.
- Headshots deal significantly more damage than body shots.
- Ammunition is limited, encouraging accurate shooting.

### Enemies

Different enemies provide different levels of difficulty:

| Enemy Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Regular Enemies | Approach and attack the player. |
| Special Enemies | More difficult to hit but can be defeated relatively quickly. |
| Boss Enemy | A powerful enemy capable of defeating the player with a single hit. |

### Combat Balance

Enemies have relatively high health, encouraging players to aim carefully.

Although enemies can take considerable damage, accurate headshots reward players with significantly higher damage.

This creates a balance between enemy durability and the player's limited shooting speed.

---

## Challenges & Obstacles

Players must overcome several obstacles to reach the end of the level.

| Challenge | Description |
|---|---|
| Enemy Spawners | Spawn enemies that the player must fight to survive. |
| Parkour | Requires players to jump across platforms and avoid obstacles. |
| Dark Environments | Limited visibility makes it harder to identify nearby enemies. |
| Sound Awareness | Players must rely on audio cues to detect approaching threats. |
| Boss Encounters | Powerful enemies require careful movement and survival skills. |

### Skills Required

- **Accuracy:** Aim for enemy heads to deal more damage.
- **Movement:** Navigate platforms and overcome parkour obstacles.
- **Awareness:** Listen for sounds to identify nearby enemies.
- **Resource Management:** Use ammunition and health carefully.
- **Survival:** Avoid enemy attacks, especially during boss encounters.

---

## Rewards

Players can find small resting areas throughout the level.

These areas provide opportunities to recover and gather supplies before continuing.

Available rewards include:

- **Health Pickups:** Restore the player's health.
- **Ammo Pickups:** Replenish ammunition.
- **Resting Areas:** Provide a safe place to recover and prepare for upcoming challenges.
